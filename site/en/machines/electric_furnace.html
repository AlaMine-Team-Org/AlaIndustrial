Electric Furnace

alaindustrial:electric_furnace
MachinesLVImplementedtier-lvprocessingmetalssmelting

Summary

The Electric Furnace is a full electrical counterpart to the vanilla furnace. It smelts everything a regular furnace can — ores, food, glass, cobblestone — but runs on EU/FE instead of fuel and works twice as fast. On top of the vanilla recipes, the mod adds its own, mainly dust → ingot, which closes the ore-doubling loop started by the Macerator.

Mini-example: grind iron ore in the Macerator to get two dusts, then drop both in here. In a few seconds you have two iron ingots — one vein, doubled, no fuel spent.

Energy

The Electric Furnace only consumes EU/FE and keeps a small buffer for smooth running.

WhatValue
Internal buffer800 EU/FE
Max input32 EU/FE/t (LV)
Consumption2 EU/FE/t while working

An LV machine — it draws up to 32 EU/FE per tick. Any surplus in the network isn't taken, nothing is damaged.

Energy comes in on the five working faces. The front face (the one the machine "looks" toward) is inert — cables don't connect there and hoppers don't work through it.

Processing

The furnace first looks for one of the mod's own recipes, and falls back to vanilla smelting if there is none. When both match the same input, the mod recipe wins. The base smelt takes ~100 ticks (~5 s) — about twice as fast as a vanilla furnace.

Interface

One screen shows the energy buffer, the progress, the input slot and the output slot.

The status line under the slots explains why the machine stands idle: "Not enough material", "No matching recipe", "Output is blocked", "No energy". An empty machine gets no line at all — an empty slot speaks for itself. The energy line does not appear the moment the buffer runs dry, but only once it has stayed empty for a while: a machine that barely has enough power runs one tick on, one tick off, and a caption blinking along with it would lie about a stall.

Block Properties

An ordinary solid block, softer than stone. Any pickaxe breaks it, but it does NOT keep its charge when broken.

Recipes

The Electric Furnace is crafted at a workbench from the mod's own Iron Furnace — it sits in the centre as the smelting core (not a vanilla furnace: this is the next step of the progression iron → Iron Furnace → Electric Furnace). Beside it, an Electronic Circuit drives the process and a Battery gives the starting charge. Silver ingots form the casing — three across the top row and three across the bottom.

The mod recipes (14 of them, all 200 EU/FE) are the mod's dust → ingot conversions (8 metals), the mod's specific ores (nickel/silver/tin/uranium/palladium), and iron tempering. For six of the eight dusts — copper, iron, gold, nickel, silver, tin — there is also a parallel plain furnace recipe for the same input/output. That isn't a violation of the rule below; it lets the Iron Furnace and the plain vanilla furnace smelt that dust too, not just the Electric Furnace. The same duplicate exists for the five raw ore smelts (nickel, silver, tin, uranium, palladium) and for iron tempering — twelve of the fourteen mod recipes in total. The Electric Furnace always resolves its own mod recipe first (see Processing above), so you never get two ingots from one dust, and the JEI/REI category lists that smelt once: the mirror of our own duplicate is dropped . Uranium and palladium dust smelt only here — unlike the other six they have no such parallel recipe: a deliberate choice, see iron_furnace.md and material_forms.md. Separately from that pair of metal duplicates: anything that is numerically the same as a vanilla smelt regardless of the mod (cobblestone → stone, sand → glass, raw copper/gold/iron → ingot) goes through the single vanilla fallback — no duplicate recipe at all. Non-metal dusts (coal, diamond, emerald, lapis) are not smelted here — the Compressor handles those.

In the recipe viewer

The Electric Furnace category in JEI/REI lists everything the machine actually smelts — the mod's own recipes and every vanilla smelt (ores, food, sand → glass …), each priced as the Electric Furnace runs it: 200 EU/FE · 5 s, not the vanilla "XP in 10 sec". Clicking the progress arrow opens that category alongside the vanilla one; clicking the Electric Furnace block in the item list does the same.

Vanilla recipes enter the category as mirrors: they are built for display only and never join the game's recipe registry — at runtime the machine still executes the live vanilla recipe. The numbers you read and the behaviour you get therefore cannot drift apart.

Every smelt is meant to be listed exactly once. The mod does not duplicate with its own recipe anything a vanilla furnace already does with the same input and output (cobblestone → stone, sand → glass, raw copper/gold/iron → ingot): those smelts live only in the vanilla fallback and reach the category as a single mirror. The Mekanism Energized Smelter and Modern Industrialization follow the same rule — a single source of truth for smelts that are numerically identical to vanilla ones (including the 200 EU/FE · 5 s price, which the mirror takes from the same setting the machine ticks).

How the invariant holds where a duplicate does exist. Twelve mod recipes carry a vanilla duplicate of their own for the Iron Furnace's sake (six dusts, five raw ores and iron tempering). The mirror of such a duplicate never reaches the category: a vanilla smelt is dropped when a mod recipe already turns every one of its input items into the same stack. The mod recipe is the one kept, and that is the choice that favours the player: its input is a tag (#c:dusts/copper), so the card covers other mods' copper dust as well, while the duplicate names one concrete item. Raw rubber is no longer included after removed its smelting recipe.

Progression

Tier: LV. The starter electric smelter and the other half of ore-doubling. Evolves into the Induction Furnace — the MV, faster, hotter evolution of smelting. Needs power from an LV source, e.g. a Generator.

Related

Macerator — produces the dust this furnace smelts back into ingots. Induction Furnace — the MV evolution of smelting. Compressor — handles the non-metal dusts this furnace can't. Generator — the power source.
