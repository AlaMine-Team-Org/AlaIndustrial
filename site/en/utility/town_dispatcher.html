Town Dispatcher

alaindustrial:town_dispatcher
UtilityLVImplementedtier-lvutilitysettlementconstruction

Summary

The Town Dispatcher is the desk from which a settlement restores its buildings. It is where the Restoration Plan is put together from three sheets, its buffer holds the materials, and Construction Drones take their blocks from there and only from there. The first building restored this way is the town hall.

Quick example: the core in the town hall is lit and the settlement is yours. Place the dispatcher inside the town hall, put the three town hall plan sheets into it and press "Assemble plan" — the plan stays in the dispatcher. Place a nest over the town hall and put a construction drone in it, fill the buffer with the blocks of the stage and press "Start" on the "Task" tab.

How it works

  1. Its own settlement. The dispatcher finds by itself the settlement whose territory it stands on (if the territories of two settlements overlap — the one whose core is closer), and never changes it after that.
  2. Only in the town hall. The dispatcher works only if it stands inside the town hall of its settlement — within its bounds. Outside it says "Not inside the town hall".
  3. One per settlement. A second dispatcher of the same settlement does not work and says "The town already has a dispatcher". The one that started working first is the one that works.
  4. The plan. The three Town Hall Plan Sheets (k/3) go into the three sheet slots. The "Assemble plan" button takes one sheet from each slot and puts a "Restoration Plan" into the plan slot. The sheets must be of one style — this settlement's style; if something is wrong, the reason is written under the button (a sheet is missing, the same sheet is in twice, the sheets come from towns of different styles, the style is another town's). A finished plan can also simply be placed into the plan slot. The assembled plan stays in the plan slot — that is the active plan, and there is no need to take it: a plan taken out (or a broken dispatcher) stops the drones, and the work stands with the reason "No restoration plan" until the plan is put back. Neither sheets nor the plan go into the buffer.
  5. Material buffer. 27 slots. The drone takes blocks from here and only from here: it does not open the settlement's chests. An item is used up at the moment the drone places the block — not when it takes off.
  6. Status. The dispatcher's face shows a radar: at rest — a quiet screen, while the settlement's drones are working — rings of signal spread out from the centre.

Who is in charge

The plan, the materials and starting construction belong to the settlement's restorer only. For a player who is not part of the settlement the dispatcher does not open at all: the chat says "The core does not recognise you: you are not part of this settlement." An operator in creative mode opens the window and sees everything, but cannot put in or take out an item, assemble a plan or start construction. While nobody has woken the settlement, anyone can manage it. The restorer starts construction with the "Start" button on the "Task" tab (it adds clearing and building the town hall to the core's queue) or in the Settlement Core's window, "Construction" tab.

Interface

The window is wider than usual (256 pixels), with the tabs as icons along the left edge, as on the Settlement Core. All text wraps onto the next line and never runs onto the inventory.

  • At the top — the block's name (on the other tabs — the tab's name), and below it on its own line the status: "No town around", "Not inside the town hall", "The town already has a dispatcher", "Waiting for work", "Drones at work" or the reason the plan did not assemble.
  • "Task" (opens first) — the plan row: three sheet slots, the "Assemble plan" button, the plan slot and the "Start" button; below it, in a frame above the inventory, a list of four steps for someone seeing the dispatcher for the first time. The list never goes past its frame: if it is taller, the mouse wheel scrolls it and a scroll bar appears on the right. Each step has a tick or a cross, and an open step has one line on what to do next:
    1. "Restoration plan" — put the 3 plan sheets in and press "Assemble plan"; once assembled — "Keep the plan here, no need to take it"; a plan of another town hall (another style) — a cross and "This plan is for another town hall";
    2. "Nests with a drone: N of M" — how many nests with a drone stand over the town hall and work, and how many the core's tier allows;
    3. "Stage materials in the buffer" — everything the current stage needs is in the buffer;
    4. "Construction" — "Press "Start"", while the button is not ready — ""Start" opens once the plan and a drone are ready"; when the jobs are queued — their state and the reason they stopped; after restoration — "The town hall stands whole, the drones look after it", and during a repair — the repair jobs.
  • The "Start" button is inactive while something is missing; its tooltip lists exactly what. Required: the settlement's restorer, a town hall that can be restored and has no job yet, the plan of this town hall in the plan slot, and at least one nest with a drone over the town hall. Materials are not required: clearing comes first and needs no blocks, so short materials are only a warning in the tooltip ("Materials are short: drones start with clearing"). The server checks the same things again when the button is pressed. Once the town hall is restored the button is inactive for good ("The town hall is restored, the drones look after it themselves"): the drones mend a damaged town hall themselves, and a repair is queued without the button.
  • "Buffer" — three rows of nine slots and a hint that drones take blocks only from here.
  • "Materials" — the town hall's current stage and a grid of the stage's item icons, like in slots. Under each icon — "have/need": red while short, with a green tick once there is enough. The green of every "ready" in the construction windows is the same calm dark green, readable on light grey. Hovering shows the usual item tooltip; the mouse wheel scrolls a long list.
  • "Journal" — what the drones have done, newest at the top, with the in-game time on every line; the mouse wheel scrolls it. A finished stage and the restoration of the town hall get lines of their own.
  • Chat messages. When a stage of the town hall is done, the player restoring the settlement gets a chat line with the [Ala Industrial] tag: the settlement's number and its core's coordinates, which stage is done, which is next and whether its materials are in the dispatcher ("Settlement No. 3 (core at 120, 64, -340): stage Walls is done. Next is Fittings: put its materials into the dispatcher."). The first restoration of the town hall has a line of its own ("the town hall is restored"); the last stage of a town hall restored earlier says it stands whole. A repair of a restored town hall is not counted in stages: one line when the repair is queued ("Settlement No. 3 (core at 120, 64, 340): the town hall is damaged, the drones are mending it.") and one when it is whole again ("…: the town hall is mended."); the same two lines go into the "Journal". Messages are sent while the player is online.
  • Below — the player's inventory.

The dispatcher takes no energy: the drones charge from the Settlement Core through their nests.
