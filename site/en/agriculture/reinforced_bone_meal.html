Reinforced Bone Meal

alaindustrial:reinforced_bone_meal
AgricultureImplementedagriculturefertilizerbone-mealitemgrowth

Summary

Reinforced Bone Meal is bone meal and biomass pressed into pellets. Ordinary bone meal does not always work: a sapling grows with a 45 % chance and only on the second successful use, a mushroom with a 40 % chance, and a crop advances by a random 2–5 stages. The reinforced kind removes the luck: right-clicking a plant gives the result on the first try, and if there can be no result, the pellet stays in your hand.

What it does

TargetResult
Sapling (vanilla, including a mangrove propagule on the ground; dark oak and pale oak only as a 2×2 square, a single one does not grow)A tree at once — both growth steps in a single use
Crop: wheat, carrots, potatoes, beetroots, torchflower, pumpkin and melon stems, cocoa, sweet berries, pitcher plantStraight to maturity
Mushroom, Nether fungus, azaleaGrows at once, with no chance roll
Grass, moss, fern, bamboo and everything else bone meal works onAs with ordinary bone meal, minus the misfire
Sea floor under water (kelp, corals)As with ordinary bone meal — and, like it, used up even if nothing grew on the floor
TrellisOne stage — only on moist farmland and in light, as with normal growth; otherwise the meal is not used up
Kok-sagyzOne stage or one block of root, with no chance roll; only in light 9 or above, as with normal growth (ordinary bone meal too); in the dark the meal is not used up
Plants from other modsThe use is repeated until the plant accepts it (up to 64 attempts); the meal does not bypass a hard condition of the plant

When the meal is not used up

The meal is consumed only if something actually grew. It stays in your hand and leaves the world

  • the tree has no room — the sapling goes back to the state it was in before the click;
  • the plant is already mature, or bone meal has no effect on it;
  • a plant from this mod or another mod did not accept the growth (soil, light, moisture);
  • the block is listed in the exclusion tag;
  • the spot is protected (a claim, spawn protection) or another mod forbade the use.

The exception is the sea floor: there the meal is used up just like ordinary bone meal, even if nothing grew.

Recipe

Crafting table, a saltire ("X") shape: four bone meal in the corners, biomass in the centre — 2 pieces.

B. B
. M.
B. B

B — bone meal, M — biomass. This is the first use of biomass in the mod.

Progression

It appears together with the fermenter: biomass drops from it by chance. It is needed wherever luck gets in the way — on a tree farm and in a planting that has to be harvested right away.
