Kok-Sagyz

alaindustrial:kok_sagyz
AgricultureImplementedagriculturerubbercropperennialrootfarmingtier-lv

Summary

Kok sagyz is a perennial rubber-bearing dandelion and the mod's first crop that yields rubber. It is the farmer's answer to oil being finite: raw rubber from a polymerizer demands irreplaceable oil, while kok sagyz grows on a farm forever. Rubber from it costs more time and less EU/FE — the crystal farm doctrine applied to a material.

The plant is a 1×3 column: a flower above ground and two root blocks down. The soil under the flower sprouts a root, and once the column reaches full depth the lower root block (the tip) can be dug out: the drop is the root item, and the plant survives and regrows the tip. Breaking the flower or the upper root kills the plant — the rubber sits in the depth, not on top.

Real-world prototype

Kok sagyz (Taraxacum kok-saghyz, the "Russian dandelion") is endemic to Central Asia: Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Uzbekistan, the Karatau range. The name comes from Kazakh "kök-sağız", "green chewing gum" — Kazakhs traditionally chewed the root latex. The rubber lives in latex vessels of the root only (5–12% dry weight in the wild, up to 25% in bred lines); the classic field test is to snap a dried root and watch rubber threads stretch between the halves.

A Soviet expedition found it in Kazakhstan in 1932; the USSR cultivated it from 1931 to ~1950, and by 1941 kok sagyz supplied roughly a third of Soviet rubber. When Japan cut the USA off from hevea, the American Emergency Rubber Project of 1942–45 sowed it across experiment stations in ~30 states — the project was called "the Manhattan Project of agriculture". Cheap synthetic rubber and liberated hevea shut it down after the war. Obtaining

Wild specimens. The first seeds come from the world: a mature kok sagyz flower (puff stage) generates in plains and savanna biomes (plains, sunflower plains, the three savannas), rarity 1/8 per chunk, up to 12 flowers scattered across it. Wild roots regrow on their own. New chunks only already generated land never grows new wild plants (see "Old worlds").

Planting. The seed goes onto a single block of soil: dirt (#minecraft:dirt), grass, podzol, mycelium, mud, sand (#minecraft:sand), tilled farmland and any modded ground in #minecraft:supports_crops. Depth is optional — the root adapts: two blocks of soil grow a two-deep column, a lone block over stone puts the tip straight under the flower. Anything that is not soil (stone, planks, a leaf) refuses the seed.

Life cycle

The plant grows on random ticks at light level ≥9 (same as vanilla crops). Flower stages (age 0..3): leaf rosette → bud → yellow flower → puff. A mature flower sprouts a root: the first soil block beneath it turns into "dirt with a root", then the root descends to depth 2 — the lower block carries the tip property. Full column = full maturity.

On wild soil (plain dirt — not farmland and not the plant's own root) both divisors are multiplied by 2 — a wild specimen grows slower than a tended one. Bone meal and the sprinkler accelerate growth but buy speed, not exemption: each application gives exactly one step (a stage or one root block), while the light floor and the "somewhere to grow" check stay in force.

After a tip harvest the plant regrows it at the same pace as root growth.

Harvesting

The one rule: dig the tip, do not tear off the flower. The tip is the lowest root block, two blocks under the flower.

ActionResult
Break the lower root block (the tip, tip=true)drop 1 × kok sagyz root + 35% chance of seeds; dirt remains where the tip was; the plant lives and regrows the tip
Break the flower or the upper root blockthe plant dies; 35% chance of seeds, no root
Remove the support under the lower rootthe column collapses — the plant dies
"Pluck the top like a dandelion" (right-click the puff)gives nothing — the rubber is in the ground, not in the puff

Tip harvesting is endless and needs no reseeding: set up a plantation, then dig it out periodically. Seeds for expansion come back from the harvest chance and from wild specimens.

Processing

The root goes into the Macerator — no new machines in the chain:

IngredientsResultEU/FE
2 × kok sagyz root1 × raw rubber + 1 × inulin (by-product)300

Inulin does not stack with rubber in one slot: the by-product is ejected from the top of the machine — put a hopper or pipe above the macerator and it leaves on its own, never blocking the work (a non-stacking by-product never stops the machine). Then the standard chain: vulcanizer (1 raw rubber + 4 sulfur dust → rubber, 400 EU/FE).

Automation

  • The scythe (shift-right-click a mature plant) digs the root tip by itself — surgically,

without harming the plant.

  • The garden drone in a mature column likewise breaks exactly the lower root block and

collects the drop.

  • The sprinkler accelerates growth step by step (see "Life cycle").

Progression

Kok sagyz is the entry into the renewable rubber branch of the LV era: cable insulation, advanced_circuit and the insulation armor set can now be farmed. The advancement "Deep Roots" (kok_sagyz_root, criterion — a root in inventory) sits in the chain between first_oil and rubber_production: the player digging a field and the player pumping oil arrive at the same rubber from two sides.

Old worlds

Wild specimens generate in new chunks only: already generated plains are never seeded with kok sagyz. In an old world the first seed cannot be found at all — but a single seed brought from another world or player grows into an endless plantation, since reseeding is no longer required.
