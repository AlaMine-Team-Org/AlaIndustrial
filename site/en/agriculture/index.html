Agriculture

Mutation incubator and the trellis.

6 pages
  • Trellis
    A two-block-tall support for climbing plants — cotton grows on it today. Placed on moist farmland, seeded by right-click. The plant roots slowly once, then fruits in an endless cycle: harvesting never destroys it.
    Implemented
  • Garden Drone Station
    An LV EU/FE-buffered station from which a BER-drawn drone lifts off and patrols farmland within a 4-block radius — tilling free dirt, planting seeds, fertilizing immature crops, and hauling ripe harvest back to the station.
    LVImplemented
  • Incubator
    A 1×2 multiblock — mechanical base plus a glass dome. Mutates items by uranium irradiation and EU/FE: duplicates, transforms or creates new ones. Mode set by a chip, outcome is probabilistic with four rarity grades.
    LVImplemented
  • Kok-Sagyz
    A perennial 'Russian dandelion' — the renewable source of rubber. A column of a flower and a two-block-deep root: dig out the root tip endlessly, the plant survives and regrows it. The root is macerated into raw rubber with inulin as a by-product.
    Implemented
  • Sprinkler
    A spinning head on a mast that sprays nutrient solution over everything growing within 4 blocks. The one machine in the mod that draws no EU/FE at all — no cable connects to it, and it runs on solution alone. Mounts on a floor or under a ceiling.
    Implemented
  • Greenhouse Controller
    A sealed room of any shape whose woken seedbeds grow real vanilla amethyst clusters. The controller floods the air inside, so a dome, a stepped pyramid or an L-shaped wing all count, and a finished greenhouse shows it by losing the seams in its shell. Water, EU/FE and a sprinkler only make it faster; none of the three is required.
    LVImplemented
